7. ITEM 5 -- THE ATTESTATION DESIGN NOTE
================================================================================

docs/2026-10-19-hank-b21-attestation-design.md. DESIGN NOTE ONLY, NO CODE, as
dispatched. One-line concept first, then Goals (5), Non-goals (6), FOUR
alternatives, and cross-cutting concerns split security / data / test plan.

THE ONE-LINE CONCEPT:
  A completed three-way reconciliation becomes an ATTESTED record only when a
  bar-admitted person signs a hash of the exact comparison that was run -- so
  "the three legs agree" and "a lawyer has attested that they agree" are two
  different stored facts, and the app can never present the first as the second.

ALTERNATIVES: (A) reuse the countersignature lock with a new profile --
RECOMMENDED; (B) self-attestation columns on the reconciliation row -- rejected
as the primary mechanism and KEPT because it is the only one that solves the
solo-practitioner case; (C) an append-only log with no lock -- rejected as
primary, KEPT AS A COMPANION since a log and a control answer different
questions; (D) do nothing and keep the disclosure -- recorded because it is the
CURRENT state and is defensible, which is why b20 shipped it.

THE SHARPEST DECISION: MODE_ALWAYS, NOT MODE_PREPARED_ONLY. C2's lock lets a
bar-admitted person write law_trusttx with no token because C2 asked only
whether a paralegal may PREPARE. Here that asymmetry DEFEATS THE PURPOSE -- an
attorney who ran the reconciliation would attest their own work with no second
party and no token, which is the shape b20 withdrew a grant to avoid.
SAIRNlegacy's F1 lock is MODE_ALWAYS and its schema says why in as many words:
a rule the owner is exempt from is not "the paperwork needs a second named
person".

NON-GOAL 1 IS THE ONE WORTH READING: an attestation records that a qualified
person LOOKED AND SIGNED. It does not and must not imply the figures are right.
The research behind the lock is explicit that even independent double-review
has a measured, non-zero failure rate. Building an attestation that reads as a
correctness guarantee would be the wrong-model-that-looks-like-compliance
failure F3 names.

THE TEST PLAN SPECIFIES THREE NEGATIVE CONTROLS, not a green suite: two
ablations (the MODE_ALWAYS branch, and the content-hash recomputation, each
expected to redden a DIFFERENT named arm) and a CLEAN control, plus SQL negative
inserts with a positive control, plus a live probe that must exit 2 COULD NOT
RUN without credentials, plus a rendered pass because the
reconciled-vs-attested distinction is visual and no static check can see it.

AND IT NAMES THE ONE QUESTION IT CANNOT ANSWER RATHER THAN GUESSING: under
MODE_ALWAYS A SOLO PRACTITIONER CANNOT ATTEST AT ALL, because there is no
second bar-admitted signer. That is not an edge case -- a large share of real
firms are one attorney plus staff, and for them the mechanism would make
attestation IMPOSSIBLE rather than REQUIRED, which is worse than the current
state where it is merely absent. Three answers are laid out and they are not
equivalent; the one with the honesty in it changes what the record CLAIMS
(single-signer, recorded as such) rather than weakening what it requires.
