THREE SOURCES NOW, AND THE CELL SAYS WHICH IT IS REPORTING:

     published_at is a string the publisher wrote, an mtime is what the
     filesystem observed, and the filesystem cannot be talked into a date.

     tip, because a beacon is a checkpoint CLAIM and the digest is the agent's

Erring tight is the safe direction: a quiet mirror reported stale costs a line
of output, where the opposite cost six days of a wrong number on a
tamper-evidence cell.
