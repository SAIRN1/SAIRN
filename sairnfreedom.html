Phase 1 scope

This build covers the foundation only — entity and licence model, officer capabilities, member roster, the three-layer fee structure and conditional waivers. Canteen, gaming compliance, disbursement and district rollup are later phases and are deliberately absent rather than stubbed.
Post Profile
Entity, organisation type, licences and eligibility facts

Entity

Entity and location are separate keys (spec 1b). An auxiliary that meets in this building is a separate entity with its own treasury and officers — never merged into this one. Sons of the American Legion are a member category of this same entity, not a separate post.

Licences

OAC 109:1-4-08 Types I/II/III gate which lawful activity the post may run. The gaming and liquor modules are coupled (spec 0e): a Chapter 2915 violation is simultaneously a liquor-permit exposure. Phase 3 builds that interlock; this phase records the facts it will branch on.

Dues & Fees
Three money types, not three prices

Fee configuration

Initiation fee and annual dues are certified figures on a liquor-permit filing. ORC 4303.17(A)(1) requires the officer certification to set forth the amount of initiation fee and yearly dues, and ORC 4301.25(A)(3) makes a false material statement in a permit application a revocation ground. Changing either raises a permit-filing flag rather than silently updating a price.

Waivers
Governance decisions with an audit trail — not discount codes

Grant a waiver

A dues waiver never waives the national per-capita. The lodge may waive its own revenue; the pass-through is owed upward for every member regardless. Waiving it silently would mean the lodge funds the difference without seeing it.
