Honest scope note: the AI step analyzes the photo and describes a grounded design direction in text (Claude vision) — it does not generate a photorealistic rendered "after" image. StoneDesk's "Visualize on Your Kitchen" proves that pattern is buildable (Stability AI image-edit, capped, needs a provisioned API key), but reusing it here needs a new landscape-specific vendor call and a new cost/cap decision Michael hasn't made for this app yet — flagged as a real Phase 2 upgrade, not silently built as if it were already live. Approval is an in-app recorded confirmation (name + timestamp), not a legally-binding e-signature service — no e-sign vendor (DocuSign etc.) is wired in, matching how DocuSign already shows as tracked-not-connected elsewhere on this platform.
