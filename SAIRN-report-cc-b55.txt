== ITEM 5 -- METHODOLOGY: PRESENCE PASSED AS PROOF ==
A check confirms a thing EXISTS, and the app reports that as the thing being RIGHT.
  Citator: a case exists at the cite -> "verified", whatever name, court or holding is claimed (A1-A3).
  Reconcile: the totals match -> "Reconciled", while a stale in-transit item hides a shortfall (M5) or money sits on the wrong client (M6).
  Deadlines: a test file exists -> the state is treated as covered, though 37/39 have never been seen to fail.
Two companion shapes in the citator:
  UNSEEN IS UNCHECKED IS UNSHOWN (F3-F5, A5): what the extractor does not recognise, or drops past six, is never checked and never displayed.
  NEAREST-KEY TRANSFER (X1): a substring fallback moves one citation's result onto another key.
