THE PATTERN b30 PREDICTED HELD. Twelve of the thirteen apps needed the same
one-line move b29 made in SAIRNdental: the bright brand token keeps every
border, accent and focus ring, and ONLY THE SURFACES UNDER WHITE TEXT move to
the darker token the app ALREADY DEFINED. No new palette was invented for any
app. --pdd was added where :hover would otherwise have collapsed into the new
resting colour; sairnmechanical already had --d and needed nothing new.

THE EXTREMES ARE WORTH NAMING. sairnmechanical's lime was 1.98:1 under white
text on seven surfaces -- the worst on the platform. sairndesign's was 4.47:1,
which fails AA by three hundredths and is exactly the kind of thing no eye can
adjudicate and no code review would ever catch.

TWO FIXES RAN THE OTHER WAY, AND SAYING SO MATTERS. sairncash is the one
DARK-themed app, so its muted text was LIGHTENED against two dark surfaces
rather than the surface being darkened; --dim had to clear both #111220 and
#1A1B2E, so it took the lighter of the two answers. And for every failure that
was TEXT ON A TINT, the text was darkened rather than the tint washed out --
lightening #FEE2E2 to #FFF6F6 would have "fixed" the ratio by deleting the
design.

TWO COLOURS HAD TO SATISFY BOTH ROLES AT ONCE. sairncode's #F87171 is both a
surface under white AND text on #fef2f2; #DF0B0B clears both at 5.01 and 4.58.
The same was true of the greens in sairnmechanical and stonedesk: #117F3A is
5.10 under white and 4.64 as text on #DCFCE7.

THE COUNTED ASSERTION EARNED ITS KEEP THREE TIMES. Every edit declares how many
occurrences it expects and REFUSES on a mismatch rather than writing:

  sairncode        expected 31, found 33   -- `grep -c` counts LINES, and two
                                              lines carry the colour twice

  stonedesk        expected 2, found 3     -- one CSS rule and two inline

Each of those would have been a half-done edit that reads exactly like a
finished one. TWO OF THEM WIDENED THE FIX DELIBERATELY AND IT IS DISCLOSED: in
sairnmechanical and stonedesk the SAIRNbiz cross-sell teal moved in all six and
all nine places rather than only the four that were findings, because the

2. ITEM 2 -- STONEDESK'S 56 UNDEFINED TOKENS: NOT A DELETION
================================================================================

CONCEPT FIRST, THEN THE SEARCH. The hypothesis was a deleted :root block. It
was wrong, and the search said so immediately:

  git log -S"--surface-2:" -- stonedesk.html   ->   NO COMMITS
  git log -S"--surface:"   -- stonedesk.html   ->   NO COMMITS

NO COMMIT EVER DEFINED THEM IN THIS FILE. They were never deleted; they were
never there. The references came in with markup copied from the ARCHIVED
SAIRNaccounting app, which does define them:

  archive/.../sairnaccounting UPLOAD TO GITHUB 911.html
     --surface:#FFFFFF      --surface-2:#EDF7F1

So 56 background declarations in StoneDesk have been invalid at computed-value
time since the day they were written -- DROPPED, not drawn in a fallback. Every
hover row, chip, message bubble and tracker surface that was meant to tint has
been rendering flat.

FIXED by defining them against StoneDesk's OWN equivalents rather than importing
the accounting palette:

  --surface:var(--card);      /* #FFFFFF */
  --surface-2:var(--stone);   /* #f0f4fb */

AND THE CHECK THAT MATTERED: re-measuring after the definitions landed produced
NO NEW contrast failures from the newly visible surfaces. A fix that makes 56
backgrounds render could easily have created 56 new text-on-tint problems; it
did not, and that was verified rather than assumed.

b30 recommended stopping the series until two company questions were settled.

IT IS NOT, AND THE REASON IS NEW: the market is already served.

  - the software field is crowded and already LADDERED BY OPERATOR SIZE:
    GorillaDesk and Housecall Pro at 1-3 technicians, FieldRoutes, PestPac and
    Briostack at 5-15 trucks, FieldRoutes as the stated default above that
  - nothing surfaced that the incumbents are documented as failing to do.

  - AND THE CORE PRIMITIVE IS ABSENT HERE: route optimisation is what the buyer
    literature says operators buy first, and routing is 0 of 17 on this
    platform. SAIRN's field apps are JOB-shaped, not ROUTE-shaped.

PRIMARY CITATIONS, Federal Register API:
  82 FR 952     doc 2016-30332   Certification of Pesticide Applicators,
                                 eff 2017-03-06 -- 5-year recertification cap,
                                 minimum age, specialised fumigation/aerial

BOTH DEADLINES ARE PAST. Unlike trucking, there is no live federal cliff. The
live layer is per-state -- one-to-three-year terms, 4 to 24 CEUs per cycle, and
renewal dates that differ per state and licence class (TN 2026-06-30, MN
2026-03-01, NC 2026-06-30/09-30, OH 2026-01-15). All of that is REPORTED and
unverified; no state register was read.

THE DENOMINATOR IS A RANGE, NOT A NUMBER: NPMA 16,565 firms, BLS 17,933
establishments, IBISWorld 31,307 businesses. Those count three different
things and a single figure would be a fabrication. 81.4% of firms run 1-2
locations, which is the one structural fact that genuinely favours SAIRN.

  - chemical|applicator|pesticide returned 3 of 17, one being SAIRNvet's

WHAT FOUR AUDITS HAVE NOW ESTABLISHED, which is the real output of the series:
the blocker has never once been market size or regulatory difficulty. It has
