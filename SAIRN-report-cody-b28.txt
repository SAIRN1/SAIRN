THE SENTENCE: a signed token proves WHO ASKED and WHEN THEY SIGNED IN. It

THE SECOND HALF IS SHARPER. The SETTLING STEP exists because "between the

    honest: both mean nobody currently employed stands behind this.

"THE TOP" IS COMPUTED, NOT LINE 1. A Python docstring must stay the first

under test. So .py goes after the docstring, located with `ast` (a regex cannot
find the end of a docstring that itself contains a triple-quote, and several do);
