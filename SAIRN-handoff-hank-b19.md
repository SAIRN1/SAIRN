### THE RESEARCH CONTRADICTS THE DISPATCH'S PREMISE, AND THE CODE SAYS SO

The dispatch's premise was *"two of the three legs are bar-admitted-only by
law."* **That is not what the rules say.** What the sources support, in the same
way `PRESCRIBER_ROLES` is supported:

* **The three legs are RECORDS, not acts** — the bank statement's adjusted
  balance, the firm's own trust ledger balance, and the SUM of the individual
  client sub-ledgers. None is an act a licence is required to perform.
* **ABA Model Rule 1.15** puts the DUTY on the lawyer and makes it
  non-delegable as a **RESPONSIBILITY**.
* **ABA Model Rule 5.3** expressly permits non-lawyer assistants under
  supervision, so the arithmetic **IS** delegable — and in practice it is.
* **The bar-admitted-only act is the SIGNATURE.** New York Rule 1.15(e) is the
  clearest: only an attorney admitted in the State may be an authorised
  signatory on an attorney trust account. That is **authorising money out** —
  exactly the act C2's two-stage lock already models.
* State bar guidance mandating periodic three-way reconciliation also puts the
  **review** on the lawyer and warns against a non-lawyer holding sole or
  unsupervised control of the trust account.

**SO THE ROUTING IS RIGHT AND THE REASON IS DIFFERENT.** A bookkeeper with
trust access and no lawyer between them and the money is the specific
arrangement that guidance warns about. But it is not because the legs need a
licence — it is because the **acts on them** do, and because the responsibility
cannot be handed over with the task.

### ONE DEFECT FOUND BY DRIVING IT, NOT BY READING IT

The first draft let a **named** verb through anywhere the role held `rw`:
`decide('law_invoices', 'bookkeeper', 'reconcile')` returned **ALLOW**, because
that row is unlocked, the verb fell through as WRITE-class like any unknown
verb, and bookkeeper holds `rw` on invoices. So a verb meaning *"perform leg (a)
or (b) of a trust reconciliation"* was granted on a resource with no legs —
**and `prepare` had the identical hole since b18 and nobody had asked.** Named
verbs are now refused off the resources that define them; an **unknown** verb
is still write-class, which is what that rule was always for, and arms N5a–N5e
are the silent half proving the narrowing caught nothing else.
