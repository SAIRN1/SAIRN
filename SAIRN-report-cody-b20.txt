2. ITEM 2 -- SECTION 45B ON VFW/LEGION CANTEEN TIPS
---------------------------------------------------
DELIVERABLE: docs/2026-09-17-sairnfreedom-tips-tax-treatment.md section 12,
APPENDED TO THE CANONICAL FILE rather than written as a new document.

WHY APPENDED AND NOT NEW: the app's own code cites that file at
sairnfreedom.html:4418, and sections 7 and 10 already carried Pub. 3386,
TD 10044, section 45B, the 501(c)(19) chain, the frozen minimum wage and the
customary-tipping split. CHECKED BEFORE WRITING ANYTHING. A second home for
the same fact is the defect that document itself warns about.

THE ANSWER, IN ONE PARAGRAPH. YES -- section 45B applies to tips reported by
VFW/Legion canteen staff, AND MEMBER TIPS COUNT, on Rev. Rul. 2003-64 and the
face of 45B(b)(2): the tips are received in connection with serving food or
beverages, and an exempt club's own members are "customers" for that test. BUT
the credit is NONREFUNDABLE and the post must first owe tax it can offset --
in practice section 511 UBIT on Form 990-T -- so a post with no UBTI gets
nothing now and only a section 39 carryforward (1 back, 20 forward). TD 10044
is IRRELEVANT to the employer credit; it governs the employee's section 224
deduction. PUB. 3386 DOES NOT ADDRESS 45B at all and routes the tip question to
Pub. 531.

WHAT WAS GENUINELY OPEN AND IS NOW CLOSED. Section 9 of that document
escalated ONE question to outside counsel and filed an index row so it would
get asked: whether FICA paid on EXEMPT-function tips can generate a credit
usable against UBIT from a DIFFERENT activity. REV. RUL. 2003-64 ANSWERS IT,
and no section above cited it:

  "Section 45B does not prohibit social clubs described in 501(c)(7) from
   claiming the credit, nor does it limit the credit to social security taxes
   paid in connection with a social club's unrelated trade or business.
   Therefore, A may calculate the 45B credit on the basis of all tips received
   by its employees, not only the tips received by employees from nonmembers."

THE GAP WAS A SEARCH GAP, NOT A REASONING GAP, and that is the reusable part.
Sections 7.1 and 10.0 list statute, regulation, DOL and Ohio sources and NOT
ONE PUBLISHED IRS RULING. A source table with no ruling line cannot find a
ruling. Both sections reasoned correctly from the statute and both said, in
terms, that the statute alone could not settle the confinement question.

TWO MORE THINGS THE RULING SETTLES.
  * MEMBER TIPS COUNT. Section 10 had left 45B(b)(2)'s "customers" wording as
    a hurdle "against this document's own exempt-function position". The
    ruling computes the credit on ALL tips and says the test was met "because
    the tips were received in connection with food and beverage service at A,
    and tipping of employees is customary at A."
  * Section 7.3's conclusion -- the credit is reachable only to the extent the
    canteen produces UBTI -- is now CONFIRMED by an IRS ruling rather than
    inferred from 38(c).

WHERE IT STOPS, stated because a ruling read too widely is worse than no
ruling.
  1. IT IS A 501(c)(7) RULING AND A POST IS USUALLY 501(c)(19). The credit
     reasoning does not depend on which subsection; the UBTI COMPUTATION does
     -- 512(a)(3) applies to (c)(7), (c)(9) and (c)(17), NOT to (c)(19). So the
     FACTS do not transfer even where the HOLDING does. AND THIS IS NOT
     ACADEMIC: Pub. 3386 says a veterans' organisation running a bar,
     restaurant or canteen for members "may be recognized as tax exempt under
     IRC 501(c)(7)", and that such clubs "are often operated in conjunction
     with IRC 501(c)(4) veterans' organizations". WHICH SUBSECTION THE CANTEEN
     SITS UNDER IS A FACT ABOUT THE POST and it changes which authority is on
     all fours. A build must not assume (c)(19).
  2. THE RULING HAS ONE ACTIVITY SERVING TWO CLASSES OF CUSTOMER; section 9.1
     posits a canteen with NO UBTI and UBTI from a DIFFERENT activity. The
     holding's rationale is activity-agnostic -- it rests on an ABSENCE in
     45B -- but the ruling does not present that pattern. So section 9 narrows
     from "unanswered" to "answered in principle, not on all fours".
  3. PUB. 3386 DOES NOT ADDRESS 45B, AND THAT IS A MEASUREMENT WITH A STATED
     LIMIT. A keyword pass over a text extraction of Pub. 3386 (Rev. 4-2018,
     Catalog 27489D) found 0 hits for 45B, 8846 and "social security", against
     73 for 501(c)(19), 52 for "unrelated" and 5 for "canteen". THE ZEROS ARE
     NOT PROOF OF ABSENCE -- the extraction recovers the publication's prose
     but not its embedded font streams, and a truncated read is
     indistinguishable from a complete one. What the pass DOES establish is
     the prose it recovered: the recordkeeping duty names tip income, and the
     reference list sends a post to "Publication 531, Reporting Tip Income"
     and never to the employer credit.

AND THE IRS'S OWN LIVE GUIDANCE PAGE CONTRADICTS THE STATUTE.
45B(b)(1)(B) freezes the floor at the minimum wage "as in effect on
January 1, 2007" -- $5.15 -- and IR-2007-155 says so in terms. Rev. Rul.
2003-64 quotes 45B(b)(1)(B) in its PRE-FREEZE form with no date, which usefully
dates the freeze to after 2003. The IRS FICA Tip Credit page, PAGE LAST
REVIEWED OR UPDATED 16-AUG-2026, says verbatim:

  "You can't claim the credit for taxes on any tips used to meet a minimum
   wage of $7.25 per hour, the federal minimum wage rate in effect on
   July 24, 2009."
  "The minimum wage basis for the FICA Tip Credit is $7.25 per hour."

THE DIRECTION OF THE ERROR MATTERS: $7.25 makes the subtraction LARGER and the
credit SMALLER, so a post following that page UNDERSTATES its credit. Not a
penalty trap -- a money trap, which is the harder kind to notice. RECORDED AS A
DOCUMENTED DISAGREEMENT WITH BOTH SIDES QUOTED AND DATED, NOT as a
determination that the page is wrong: a Treasury position in guidance is not
something this platform overrules from a statute reading. It becomes a one-line
question with two citations for the same professional section 9 was already
going to.

AND THE DISPATCH'S PAIRING IS WRONG IN A SPECIFIC WAY, re-stated because it is
the natural pairing to make. TD 10044 implements section 224 -- the
INDIVIDUAL's deduction -- and its occupation list is OCCUPATION-KEYED and
ESTABLISHMENT-AGNOSTIC. NOTHING ON THE TREASURY TIPPED OCCUPATION CODE LIST
MAKES AN EMPLOYER ELIGIBLE FOR 45B, AND NOTHING OFF IT MAKES ONE INELIGIBLE.
The two provisions share the word "tips" and nothing else. One datum added
since section 10 was written: the final rule ADDS three occupations -- Visual
Artists (509), Floral Designers (510), Gas Pump Attendant (810) -- and removes
none.

THE BUILD DOES NOT CHANGE. Section 7.3's warning stands: a field that invites a
post to chase the credit pushes the decision the wrong way round, because

POST https://sairn.vercel.app/api/sairncash/checkout {"email":"..."}. It was
