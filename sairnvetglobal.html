💉 Anesthesia & Capture Protocols
Species-specific anesthesia protocols with AI guidance. The most dangerous part of zoo veterinary medicine -- Claude knows the critical risks for every species. No other platform has this.
⚠ CRITICAL SAFETY INFORMATION
Elephant: ALWAYS have diprenorphine reversal on site. Head-down positioning = cerebral edema. Minimize anesthesia time.
Giraffe: MOST dangerous large animal to anesthetize. Capture myopathy. Must keep head elevated. Etorphine ONLY with immediate reversal.
Great Ape: Human respiratory pathogens = lethal. Full PPE mandatory. B virus from macaque bites = medical emergency.
Rhino: Black rhino most sensitive to etorphine. Always have reversal. Never leave animal unattended.

🦠 Zoonotic Disease Alerts
Diseases transmissible between animals and humans. Every zoo employee needs to know what their species can transmit. AI generates species-specific zoonotic risk briefings.
CRITICAL ZOONOTIC RISKS -- KNOW BEFORE YOU ENTER
🦍 Macaques: Herpes B virus -- FATAL in humans. Any bite = emergency protocol. Wash wound 15 min, call physician immediately.
🐘 Elephants: Tuberculosis (M. tuberculosis) -- annual trunk wash screening required. Salmonella, Herpesvirus.
🦁 Big Cats: Toxoplasma, Cryptosporidium, Salmonella, Pasteurella from bites.
🐋 Cetaceans: Brucella (B. ceti) -- handle all samples with gloves. Respiratory Brucella from blowholes.
🦅 Raptors: Chlamydiosis (psittacosis) -- mask when handling. Histoplasma in old roost sites.

🌿 SSP Breeding Plan
Species Survival Plan management. AZA breeding recommendations, transfer coordination, genetic diversity targets. Claude knows SSP protocols for all managed species.
SSP (Species Survival Plan) is an AZA program managing captive populations of endangered species. Breeding pairs are recommended based on Mean Kinship -- the goal is to retain maximum genetic diversity from wild founders. SAIRNvet Global tracks your animals' SSP status and breeding recommendations.
🔬 Genetic Diversity Tracker
Track genetic diversity of your collection. Mean Kinship scores, founder representation, population viability. Critical for long-term species survival.
Genetic diversity target: most SSPs aim to retain 90% of wild genetic diversity for 100 years. Each wild-caught founder is irreplaceable. Over-representation of any one founder reduces overall diversity.
⚖ USDA APHIS Compliance
Animal Welfare Act compliance. Annual inspection preparation. Elephant tuberculosis screening. Exhibitor license management.
USDA APHIS inspection: minimum space, veterinary care, enrichment, and recordkeeping standards apply to all warm-blooded animals in public exhibition. Violations: up to $10,000/day. SAIRNvet Global generates your inspection preparation checklist.
🌍 CITES Permit Manager
Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species. Track permits for all CITES-listed animals in your collection. International transfers require CITES documentation.
CITES Appendix I species: commercial trade prohibited. Any international movement requires CITES permits from both exporting AND importing country. Violations = federal criminal charges. Never move CITES-listed animals internationally without verified permits.

AZA accreditation is the gold standard in zoo operations. It covers animal care and welfare, veterinary programs, conservation, education, finance, governance, and physical facilities. SAIRNvet Global tracks your compliance and prepares you for the inspection.
