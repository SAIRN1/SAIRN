ITEM 1 -- THE ACK STORE, DECIDED BY CHAT

  docs/2026-10-14-ai-safeguards-spec.md s7.5 (new).
  THE RULING: the acknowledgment lives on the EMPLOYEE LOGIN RECORD -- the two
  *_employee_auth columns, ai_notice_version and ai_notice_acked_at.
  api/ai-notice.js IS THE LOSER and is to be DELETED, not left unwired.
  THIS IS NOT A RE-OPENING OF MICHAEL'S DECISION, IT IS ITS CONSEQUENCE.
  s1(a) and s2.1 have said since this document was written that the ack is
  "stored under that employee's login", and the credential row IS the login.
  Five reasons recorded; the first would be enough on its own:
    1. the credential row IS the login. A side table keyed by (license_hash,
       employee_id) is a COPY of that row's identity, and a copy can disagree.
    2. one read on a path that already runs -- the login handler already
       SELECTs that row for the PIN, active and role.
    3. deactivation and deletion stay coherent for free. A separate ack table
       outlives a deleted employee and becomes an orphan nobody reaps, and
       this platform has 14 orphaned lock files to show what that looks like.
    4. cross-tenant safety is already proven there: license_hash scopes the
       row, and T5 (cross-tenant ack) is the highest-value bug in the feature.
    5. two columns against an endpoint plus a table plus a migration.
  WHAT THE LOSER WAS RIGHT ABOUT, recorded so the deletion does not throw it
  away: a dedicated endpoint is the natural home if the notice ever has to be
  acknowledged by somebody who is NOT an employee of a licence. That is s1(b)
  owner acceptance, which s2.2 puts on license_keys and s7.1 rules is not a
  runtime gate. A non-employee ack would be a NEW decision with a new store.
  ROUTED TO hank: delete the endpoint and its route; if a table shipped, copy
  rows on (license_hash, employee_id) and DROP IT IN A LATER COMMIT than the
  copy -- a drop in the same commit leaves nothing to re-run the copy from; a
  row matching no credential row is a FINDING, printed, not dropped;
  CURRENT_NOTICE_VERSION stays one constant. cody's T1-T6 are unchanged and
  gain one arm: api/ai-notice.js is gone and nothing imports it.
