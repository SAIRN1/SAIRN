ITEM 2 -- THE CARVE-OUT, AND WHERE THE RESEARCH DISAGREES
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  CONCEPT, STATED BEFORE THE CODE:
    the two trust-side reconciliation legs become reachable by a NEW NAMED
    VERB -- `reconcile` -- granted to the LEGAL-SIDE roles only (owner /
    attorney / paralegal) and NEVER to bookkeeper; a plain `read` on those two
    resources stays refused, so the hard lock still closes every other route.

  THE DISPATCH'S PREMISE WAS: "two of the three legs are bar-admitted-only by
  law." THAT IS NOT WHAT THE RULES SAY, and reporting that is the deliverable
  of doing research rather than citing it.

    * THE THREE LEGS ARE RECORDS, NOT ACTS -- the bank statement's adjusted
      balance, the firm's own trust ledger balance, and the SUM of the
      individual client sub-ledgers. None is an act a licence is required to
      perform.
    * ABA MODEL RULE 1.15 puts the DUTY on the lawyer and makes it
      non-delegable as a RESPONSIBILITY.
    * ABA MODEL RULE 5.3 expressly permits non-lawyer assistants under
      supervision, so the ARITHMETIC IS DELEGABLE -- and in practice it is.
    * THE BAR-ADMITTED-ONLY ACT IS THE SIGNATURE. New York Rule 1.15(e): only
      an attorney admitted in the State may be an authorised signatory on an
      attorney trust account. That is AUTHORISING MONEY OUT -- exactly the act
      C2's two-stage lock already models.
    * State bar guidance mandating periodic three-way reconciliation also puts
      the REVIEW on the lawyer and warns against a non-lawyer holding sole or
      unsupervised control of the trust account.

  SO THE ROUTING IS RIGHT AND THE REASON IS DIFFERENT. A bookkeeper with trust
  access and no lawyer between them and the money is the specific arrangement
  that guidance warns about. But it is not because the legs need a licence --
  it is because the ACTS ON THEM do, and because the responsibility cannot be
  handed over with the task.

  WHAT SHIPPED: LAW_RECONCILE_ACTION, LAW_RECONCILE_LEGS, LAW_RECONCILE_ROLES,
  LAW_RECONCILE_ATTESTATION, isReconcileLeg(), a second exception inside the
  trust lock, a named-verb narrowing, and a bookkeeper-specific refusal that
  says whose work it is.

  WHAT DID NOT SHIP, AND IS THE REAL DECISION-NEEDED:
  LAW_RECONCILE_ATTESTATION carries required_by_rule:true and
  implemented:false. The rules require the LAWYER TO REVIEW AND APPROVE the
  reconciliation and SAIRNlaw records no attestation anywhere, so the app can
  now produce a reconciliation a PARALEGAL ran with nothing recording that a
  bar-admitted person looked at it. Two booleans rather than a sentence, so
  "we know and have not built it" cannot decay into "it is fine".
