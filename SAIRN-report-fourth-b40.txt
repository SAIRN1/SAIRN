2. ITEM 2 -- THE EUR-LEX ROUTE, AND WHAT IT BOUGHT

  docs/cloud-research/2026-10-20-eu-law-route-and-the-three-audits-eu-limbs.md

  THE PROBLEM, THREE BATCHES RUNNING. eur-lex.europa.eu HTML pages returned an
  EMPTY BODY to every attempt: b37 on the AMLR summary, b38 on the AI Act across
  THREE URL forms, b39 again. Each audit reported that no EU instrument could
  be cited, and I twice wrote that fixing the route was worth more than the next
  audit.

  THE FIX IS THE OFFICIAL MACHINE INTERFACE, NOT A WORKAROUND. The EU
  Publications Office exposes CELLAR with HTTP content negotiation on a CELEX
  identifier:

    GET http://publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/<CELEX>
    Accept: application/xhtml+xml
    Accept-Language: eng

    32024R1689  AI Act  -> HTTP 200, 1,257,134 bytes
    32024R1624  AMLR    -> HTTP 200, 1,165,026 bytes

  Both 303 to a Cellar resource, which is the documented behaviour of the
  identifier service. NO BOT WALL IS INVOLVED AND NONE IS CIRCUMVENTED. This is
  the EU equivalent of the eCFR renderer API that b38 and b39 used after eCFR's
  web pages 302'd to unblock.federalregister.gov, and the rule was the same both
  times: STOP AT THE WALL, FIND THE PUBLISHED MACHINE INTERFACE. Re-runnable in
  scratchpad/b40/eu_fetch.py.

  THE HEADLINE: THE AI ACT IS NOT A FUTURE CLIFF. Article 113, read from source:

    "It shall apply from 2 August 2026. However: (a) Chapters I and II shall
     apply from 2 February 2025; (b) Chapter III Section 4, Chapter V, Chapter
     VII and Chapter XII and Article 78 shall apply from 2 August 2025, with the
     exception of Article 101; (c) Article 6(1) and the corresponding
     obligations in this Regulation shall apply from 2 August 2027."

  Done at Brussels, 13 June 2024. TODAY IS 2026-10-20, SO THE GENERAL
  APPLICATION DATE IS ELEVEN WEEKS PAST. All three prior audits treated the AI
  Act as something ahead. Only Article 6(1) -- the Annex I safety-component
  limb -- is still future, at 2 August 2027.

  AND THE ANSWER FOR SAIRNCODE IS A NEGATIVE ONE, ON THE ACTOR TEST RATHER THAN
  THE SUBJECT MATTER. Annex III point 5, read verbatim:

    5(a)  "by public authorities or on behalf of public authorities" to evaluate
          eligibility for essential public assistance "including healthcare
          services"
    5(b)  creditworthiness, except fraud detection
    5(c)  "risk assessment and pricing in relation to natural persons in the
          case of life and health insurance"
    5(d)  emergency call triage and dispatch, including "emergency healthcare
          patient triage systems"

  A dental or medical practice coding its OWN claims is not a public authority,
  is not pricing insurance, and is not triaging emergencies. Article 6(1) needs
  an Annex I product under third-party conformity assessment. SO SAIRNCODE IS
  NOT ANNEX III HIGH-RISK.

  IT IS STATED AS A BOUNDARY RATHER THAN A CLEARANCE, AND THAT IS THE USEFUL
  PART: the moment SAIRNcode were sold to, or operated on behalf of, A PAYER OR
  A PUBLIC AUTHORITY making eligibility or pricing determinations, 5(a) or 5(c)
  would engage and the full high-risk regime would follow. That is a COMMERCIAL
  line, not a technical one, and a vertical sale crosses it without anybody
  filing a code change.

  WHICH ALSO MEANS b38's B1 ARTEFACT IS RIGHT FOR THE WRONG-SHAPED REASON. It
  was built against 45 CFR 170.315(b)(11), a US certification criterion. It is
  also, almost item for item, what AI Act Article 11 / Annex IV technical
  documentation would ask for if 5(a) or 5(c) ever engaged -- intended purpose,
  intended users, foreseeable misuse, training-data provenance, validation,
  performance metrics, post-market monitoring. ONE ARTEFACT, TWO REGIMES.

  AMLR ARTICLE 90 CONFIRMS b37's DATES FROM SOURCE rather than from a snippet:

    "It shall apply from 10 July 2027, except in relation to obliged entities
     referred to in Article 3, points (3)(n) and (o), to which it shall apply
     from 10 July 2029."

  b37 asserted exactly that from a search snippet; b38 and b39 then refused to
  cite it. IT WAS RIGHT, and now it is sourced -- which is the difference
  between a figure that survives a reviewer and one that does not.

  AND THE PRIVILEGE CARVE-OUT HAS A CLAUSE NO AUDIT HAD. The carve-out covers
  information received from a client in the course of ascertaining their legal
  position or defending them in proceedings -- "whether such information is
  received or obtained BEFORE, DURING OR AFTER such proceedings". And the

    "Such knowledge and purpose can be inferred from objective factual

  THE CARVE-OUT IS NOT SELF-CERTIFYING. b38's sairnlaw audit flagged AML as a
