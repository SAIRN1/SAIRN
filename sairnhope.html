Postvention Intelligence
When the unthinkable happens, the school community is at risk. Suicide contagion is real — and the school response in the days and weeks after a loss determines whether more students are at risk. SAIRNhope generates the complete postvention response — staff communication, student support, parent outreach, media guidance, and the memorial policy that protects vulnerable students.
Immediate staff communication and briefing documentation
Student support protocol — who needs immediate attention
Parent and community communication — safe messaging
Media response guidance — what to say and what never to say
Memorial policy guidance — protecting from contagion
Long-term community healing plan documentation
