What SAIRN Health Does

5 modes.
One subscription.

Every mode powered by Claude — the world's most capable writing and reasoning AI.

01
🩺

Symptom Intelligence

Describe how you feel in plain language. Claude provides measured, informed context about possible causes, urgency indicators, and whether and how quickly to seek care — without diagnosing you or sending you into a panic spiral.

Try it
"I've had a headache every day for two weeks. What should I know before calling my doctor?"
02
📋

Medical Document Translation

Paste any lab result, doctor's note, discharge summary, or EOB. Claude translates every number, abbreviation, and medical term into language you can actually understand — and tells you what matters.

Try it
"My TSH is 4.8 and T4 is 0.9. What does this mean in plain English?"
03
💊

Medication Intelligence

Understand what any medication does, why it was prescribed, what side effects to watch for, what to avoid while taking it, and what signs that it is working — before you take the first pill.

Try it
"My doctor prescribed lisinopril. What is it, why would she prescribe it, and what should I watch for?"
04
🏥

Doctor Appointment Preparation

Tell Claude what is going on and Claude helps you articulate it clearly, prepares 10-15 relevant questions to ask, and makes sure you do not leave the appointment without the answers you actually need.

Try it
"I have a cardiology appointment tomorrow about irregular heartbeat. Help me prepare."
05
🥗

Evidence-Based Wellness

Personalized guidance on sleep, nutrition, exercise, and stress management — grounded in evidence, adapted to your specific situation, and prioritized so you know what change to make first.

Try it
"I am 50, have pre-diabetes, and work from home. What should I focus on first?"

✉️

Healthcare Communication

Draft messages to your doctor, appeal insurance denials, write patient portal messages, and request records — in the language that gets you the response you need.
