How it works
Just talk. That is it.
1
Say what is on your mind
Anything. Something happened at school today. You had a fight with your best friend. You are feeling something you cannot name. You just need someone to listen. Start anywhere. SAIRNfriend will be there.
2
SAIRNfriend listens and responds with care
Not with advice you did not ask for. Not with judgment. Not with "have you tried just being positive." With real, thoughtful responses that show you were heard — and that you matter.
3
Together we find the path forward
Sometimes that means helping you find the words to have a hard conversation with someone you love. Sometimes it means connecting you to someone who can help. Sometimes it just means not being alone tonight.

SAIRNfriend will never leave you alone with it. The moment things become unsafe, we connect you immediately to 988 — the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline — where a real human being is waiting for your call, any time, always. Your safety is the only thing that matters.
