📈 Health Trend Monitor
AI watches vitals, weight, ADL scores, and appetite over time. Flags decline before crisis. The feature that prevents hospitalizations.
WHY THIS MATTERS
75% of ALF residents who end up in the ER had detectable decline in the preceding weeks. Weight loss of 5% over 30 days is a survey deficiency trigger AND a predictor of hospitalization. This panel catches it before the family does.
🍽 Nutrition & Dining Management
Dietary profiles, therapeutic diets, allergens, texture modifications, weekly weigh-ins, and AI nutrition flags. A 5% weight loss in 30 days is a survey deficiency — catch it first.
🩹 Wound Care Tracker
Pressure ulcers, skin tears, wounds, and tissue injuries. Braden Scale risk assessment, wound staging, measurements, treatment log. Top F-tag deficiency category nationally.
SURVEY ALERT
Pressure ulcer prevention and wound care documentation are among the top F-tag citations nationally. Every wound must be staged, measured, photographed (when possible), and have a treatment order. Undocumented wounds = automatic deficiency.
😣 Pain Assessment Log
Numeric rating, PAINAD scale for non-verbal residents, documentation every shift for residents with pain. Unmanaged pain is a dignity violation and a survey deficiency.
📜 Advance Care Planning
POLST/MOLST status, advance directives, hospice eligibility, and AI-guided family conversation support. Written advance directives produce 3x better emotional outcomes at end of life.
WHY THIS MATTERS — THE HARDEST CONVERSATION MADE EASIER
25% of ALF residents die in place. Families who have had advance care planning conversations report lower guilt, less regret, and better peace of mind. Residents with written directives are less likely to die in the ER hooked to machines they never wanted. This panel facilitates the conversation before the crisis.
🔔 Change of Condition Workflow

📊 QAPI — Quality Assurance & Performance Improvement
State-required Quality Assurance and Performance Improvement program. AI generates your monthly QAPI report from facility data. No more manual compilation — done automatically from your SAIRNcare data.
WHAT IS QAPI?
QAPI is a federal and state requirement — a systematic approach to identifying and fixing care problems. Facilities must demonstrate they track quality indicators, identify problems, implement solutions, and verify improvement. Survey teams now require documented QAPI meetings and action plans. Without them: deficiency citation.
