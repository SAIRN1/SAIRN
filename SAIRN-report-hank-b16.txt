--------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE DEFECT IS THE MIRROR OF b15's FIX AND b15 NAMED IT. F1 made

of 60 minutes and 2000 cents per visit per correction. Nothing anywhere

api/_lib/sen-evv-clock.js  effectiveClockAsOf(row, field, asOfISO). The
  approval rule stays in ONE function; effectiveClock is now that function with
  asOf null, so every existing caller is byte-for-byte unchanged in behaviour.

api/_lib/sen-payroll.js  optional closed_periods [{period_start, period_end,
  paid_at}]. A CLOSED period recomputes to its AS-PAID basis. An OPEN period
  posts an ADJUSTMENT LINE for any visit in a closed period whose approval
  landed after that period's paid_at -- IN THE PERIOD CONTAINING THE APPROVAL,
  which is what stops it posting in every period that follows forever.

api/sd-data.js  the payroll branch reads payroll_closed_periods from
  sen_settings and NEVER from the caller's payload. A caller able to declare a

basis AND is counted in undatable_corrections, per caregiver and per period. A
closed-period record whose paid_at itself cannot be read REFUSES the run,
UNDATED_CLOSED_PERIOD. A FAILED read of the list is 503
CLOSED_PERIODS_UNREADABLE, because treating it as "no period is closed" would
silently unfreeze every paid period -- the louder of the two failures, so it is
the one that refuses. A 404/400 is an honest empty.

THREE NUMBERS AND NOT ONE. gross_cents keeps its existing meaning (the FLSA
weeks in the period run), adjustment_cents is what a frozen period owes and
this period pays, total_payable_cents is the sum. Folding the adjustment into
gross_cents would put a closed week's hours into an open week's overtime
computation, which is the freeze defeated one step removed.
