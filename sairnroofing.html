Company Damage Thresholds
Nothing is pre-filled here on purpose. The often-quoted “8 hits in a 10×10 test square” is a trade convention, not a law — it varies by carrier, state and policy. Enter the number your company works to and say where it comes from. Without one, an assessment refuses rather than assume.
Slope Damage Evidence
This does not decide whether the roof gets replaced — the carrier decides that. It reports, slope by slope, whether the evidence you recorded meets the threshold your company configured, and shows that threshold and its source beside every result. Carriers total individual slopes, so there is no whole-roof verdict here and there will not be one.

Warranty Tier
Name where this tier and its certification condition come from in your programme agreement. A tier with no source is reported as unusable rather than being judged — this app does not hold any manufacturer’s terms and will not act as though it does. Leave the programme id blank for a standard warranty any installer may offer. It must match a programme id on the Certifications panel.
Register a Roof
Register within is the deadline from your programme agreement, and it is stored on this roof rather than on the tier — so renegotiating later never silently re-dates a roof you already installed. Leave it blank if you have not confirmed it: the board will say no deadline stated, which is honest, instead of counting down to a date nobody checked. If you enter both a coverage term and an end date and they disagree, the certificate date wins and the disagreement is shown rather than hidden.
