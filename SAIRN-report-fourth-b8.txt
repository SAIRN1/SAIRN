================================================================================

  baseline   EXIT 1, 7 passed, 11 FAILED

THE CAUSE IS SINGLE, which is the branch the brief asked me to take if it was.
Arm 1 reported:

  "the transport REJECTS on a timeout -- every await caller would throw out of
   its handler and skip the toast. The defect relocated, not fixed."

TRUE OF THE HARNESS AND FALSE OF THE APP. stonedesk.html's sdData() catches,
calls sdDataFailed() and `return null` -- read at the catch tail, not inferred.
What the harness did was load sdData, sdDataFailed and sdFetchTimeoutSignal into
its vm and NOT the module-level state sdData writes: it sets
`_sdWriteOk[resource] = false` on entry. So the real function threw
`_sdWriteOk is not defined` INSIDE the vm, never reached its own catch, and never
reached the `return null` these arms exist to assert.

  arm 2's message was literally `_sdWriteOk is not defined`, and so were the
  other nine. ELEVEN FAILURES, ONE CAUSE.

THE FIX LOADS THE FOUR MAPS FROM THE REAL FILE BY NAME -- `_sdAuthRefused`,
`_sdReadFailed`, `_sdLastStatus`, `_sdWriteOk` -- and ASSERTS each declaration
exists rather than substituting `{}`. A stub would let the app rename or reshape
one of them and keep this suite green, which is the same seam defect one level
in. A declaration that goes missing now takes the suite RED.
