4. ITEM 2 -- THE MODULE IS DONE; THE DISPATCH IS BLOCKED
================================================================================

4.1 WHAT LANDED

  api/_lib/routes.js        the module -- pure core, injected edges
  api/_lib/routes.test.js   56 arms, 56 passing, two sabotage controls
  sql/rt_routes_schema.sql  NOT RUN against Supabase
  api/_resources/routes.js  the registry, plus one line in index.js

IT IS A RECOVERY PLUS THREE CORRECTIONS, NOT AN INVENTION. Commit 35169ea2
(2026-06-18, never an ancestor of main) already had a nearest-neighbour
ordering over Google Route Matrix drive times. The three corrections:

  (1) saved_seconds IS NULLABLE AND NULL MEANS NOT COMPUTED. The original
      floored it at Math.max(0, ...), so an unmeasurable route and an
      already-optimal one both rendered "0 min" and a reader could not tell
      which they had. Section 1 of the suite drives BOTH cases and proves they
      now differ -- and a NEGATIVE saving is returned as-is rather than
      clamped, because a heuristic can genuinely be worse than a human's order
      and storing that is learning something true.
  (2) basis IS A THREE-STATE ANSWER. 'drive-matrix', 'great-circle', and
      'unavailable' -- where unavailable LEAVES THE STORED ORDER UNTOUCHED and
      returns order:null, so "unchanged" can never be read as "confirmed
      optimal". Eight distinct reason values, because "could not optimise" with
      no reason is exactly what sent a StoneDesk reader to check the wrong
      Vercel environment variable for four months.
  (3) `locked`. A real day has fixed appointments, and an optimiser that moves
      a 9 AM commitment to save four minutes of driving is worse than no
      optimiser.

ALSO DELIBERATE, AND NAMED SO IT IS NOT MISTAKEN FOR AN OMISSION: no assumed
speed appears anywhere in the file. A straight-line distance therefore cannot
become a fabricated drive time, and the great-circle basis carries NO
saved_seconds at all. The accuracy discipline is lifted from
sairngrounds.html:2793 rather than re-derived -- error bands are ADDED, never
averaged, with a 25 m combined ceiling.

THE SUITE'S SHAPE IS THE POINT. Most of it is about the third state, not the
ordering: the ordering is a nearest-neighbour loop and it is easy; the thing
that has actually gone wrong on this platform is a "could not run" arriving
dressed as a result. Every refusal is driven in BOTH directions -- a validator
that refuses everything passes a one-sided suite -- and section 9 holds two
SABOTAGE CONTROLS that fail if either the null-saving rule or the
never-echo-the-input rule is reinstated.
