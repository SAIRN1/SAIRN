1.2 MY DECISION -- THE STRAIGHT-TIME FLOOR. FINDING H2 (HIGH): IT IS VIOLATED.

THE RULE I AM SETTING: an adjustment line is straight time at the rate in force
on the service date, and that is acceptable AS A FLOOR -- BUT AN ADJUSTMENT MUST
NEVER BE NEGATIVE. A downward approved correction must not reduce a later
period's pay.

DRIVEN, section C, against the real api/_lib/sen-payroll.js:

  UPWARD   (clock_in moved 1h earlier) -> adjustment_cents= 2000  delta=[ 60]  total_payable_cents= 2000
  DOWNWARD (clock_in moved 1h later)   -> adjustment_cents=-2000  delta=[-60]  total_payable_cents=-2000

`b.adjustment_cents += dCents` has no sign guard and
`total_payable_cents = gross + b.adjustment_cents`. SO A SCHEDULER APPROVING A
DOWNWARD CORRECTION SILENTLY DEDUCTS $20 FROM A LATER PERIOD'S WAGES.

THE RATIONALE, because the decision was asked for and not just the rule. An
adjustment line exists to PAY for work already performed and underpaid. A
negative adjustment is not an adjustment; it is A WAGE DEDUCTION TO RECOVER AN
OVERPAYMENT, and that is a separate regulated act with its own conditions -- an
employer may recoup an overpayment under the FLSA but not where the deduction
takes a workweek below the minimum wage, and many states additionally require the
employee's written authorisation. A PAYROLL RUN MUST NOT PERFORM ONE AS A SIDE
EFFECT OF AN APPROVAL, with no notice, in a period the employee has not been told
about. It is also the mirror of the harm F1 closed: F1 was sixty minutes unpaid
BY OMISSION; this is sixty minutes removed BY COMMISSION, which is worse because
somebody chose it. AND IT PAYS BELOW STRAIGHT TIME IN MICHAEL'S LITERAL WORDS:
the later period's own work is paid at gross minus 2000 cents.
