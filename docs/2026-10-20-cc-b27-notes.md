## 2. THE GATE, AND THE CONFLICT IT EXPOSED

`--max-lines` defaults to **30 for the digest and uncapped for the handoff and
report**, because content pushed out of a digest has to have somewhere to go.
Over the cap is a **finding, and the upload still happens** — a decision, not
an oversight: the failure that tool exists for is cc's b6, where nothing was
uploaded and the batch was reported closed, and a missing deliverable is worse
than a long one. Exit 1 says do not mark the batch closed. `--max-lines 0`
disables it and **says so in the output**, because a cap silently not checked
is the same defect one layer out.

Criteria lock **45/45** (was 36), bumped to `CRITERIA_VERSION 2026-10-20.1`.
The nine new arms include the boundary in both directions, the
`splitlines`-vs-`wc -l` off-by-one stated as a fixture rather than a comment,
and an **ablation**: name, first line, bytes and sha256 all pass on a 319-line
digest, which is why none of the four existing checks could have caught this.

Driven end to end against a temp directory, four behaviours each verified:
a 41-line digest is a finding and **still lands in the folder**; exactly 30
passes; `--max-lines 0` passes it and prints the note; `--max-lines 5` caps
every kind and the handoff's message says so rather than calling itself a
routing document.

**THE CONFLICT — NEEDS MICHAEL'S CALL, NOT RESOLVED HERE.** There are now
**two digest ceilings and they are not the same number**:

| ceiling | source | enforced |

| **150 words** | `tools/digest_lint.py`, `MAX_WORDS` | exits 1, **no gate runs it** |
| **30 lines** | the dispatches, now `CLAUDE.md` | `drive_upload_verify --max-lines` |

the 150: a tool's ceiling is Michael's to change, and quietly dropping it would
make the sweep the thing that introduced the drift. `AGENTS.md` now states both
with the measurements beside them.
