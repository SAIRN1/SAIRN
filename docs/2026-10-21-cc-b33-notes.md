## 5. Pay-period reopen: THE IMPLEMENTATION DOES NOT MATCH THE DECISION

**Michael's decision:** the owner, plus anyone the owner has designated for
payroll (HR, secretary, assistant, management), configurable per owner. Not
owner-only, and not a fixed role list.

**The code** (`api/sen-auth.js:93`):
`SEN_PERIOD_CLOSE_ROLES = roleSet({ owner: true, billing: true })`. That one
set gates both close and reopen (`api/sd-data.js:8270`).

It is **not owner-only**, so the "fix if owner-only" condition did not fire,
and nothing was changed. But it **is a fixed role list**, with no per-owner
designation. That contradicts the decision in both directions:

- a billing employee the owner did NOT designate can close and reopen;
- an HR person or assistant the owner DID designate cannot.

**The change needed.** It touches `api/` (hank's SAIRNsenior area, excluded
from this claim), so I routed it to hank on my status row:

1. A per-agency designation the owner alone can write, e.g. a `sen_settings`
   key `payroll_designees: [employee_id, ...]`.
2. The close and reopen gate becomes `role === 'owner' ||
   designees.includes(session.employee_id)`.
3. A designation read that **fails** answers owner-only, and says so. Treating
   it as "no designees" is not the same thing.
4. Arms: owner; a designated non-billing employee allowed; an undesignated
   billing employee refused; an unreadable designation list.

**Withdrawn:** my b31 recommendation of owner-only reopen is superseded.
