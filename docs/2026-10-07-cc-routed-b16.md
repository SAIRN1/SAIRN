### The recommendation, and why the obvious fix is wrong

**The obvious fix — delete the four stale records — fights a stated invariant.** The
register's own `merge_policy` says: *"This ledger is append-only across four clones

this file, in every clone. That is a real cost paid by three other sessions, and it
is not mine to impose.

**Re-seating is also wrong**, and this is the thing `--reseat` was trying to do:
pointing the stale record at the destination sha produces two records with
identical `(commit, summary)`, which is exactly what `--check` refuses as a
duplicate. The fix I landed in `6860aee4` makes it refuse to do this rather than
doing it and reporting success.

**PROPOSED, in preference order:**

**(A) SUPERSEDE, do not delete — the one I recommend.** Add one field to each stale
record, `superseded_by: "<destination sha>"`, and teach `--check`'s duplicate test
to skip a record carrying it. Append-only is preserved (nothing is removed), the
dead pointer stops double-counting, and the history of the rebase stays visible —
which is worth keeping, because *why* there are four duplicates is a fact about how
four clones rebase, not noise. Needs a small change in `tools/defect_register.py`
and that file plus the register are **cody's call**, not mine.

**(B) DELETE the four stale records, in ONE commit, with the rebase-resolver cost
accepted out loud.** Cheapest to do, loses nothing factual, and the next rebase
conflict on this file will need a human. Acceptable only if somebody decides that
trade deliberately.

**(C) LEAVE THEM and correct the figures at the point of quotation.** Cheapest of
