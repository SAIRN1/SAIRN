## Why this ask exists

The Colorado gate (`docs/sairnlaw-colorado-deadline-seed-gate.md`) came out
split: the **statutes** are freely and officially available as OLLS PDFs on
`leg.colorado.gov` (C.R.S. § 24-11-101 was read verbatim from one), but the
**consolidated current Colorado Rules of Civil Procedure** are not published
anywhere free — the Judicial Branch offers only per-amendment rule-change PDFs,
and the maintained text sits on the LexisNexis public-access site behind an
"I Agree" dialog.

The public-access site's own page says:

> The Office of Legislative Legal Services can provide electronic copies of
> specified portions of the Colorado Revised Statutes without charge.

The question is simply whether that service extends to the **court rules**. If
it does, Colorado is immediately readable and the terms question never arises.

## Draft

**Subject:** Request for electronic copy of the Colorado Rules of Civil Procedure

Dear Mr. Carr,

I am writing to ask whether the Office of Legislative Legal Services is able to
provide an electronic copy of the **Colorado Rules of Civil Procedure** — in
particular C.R.C.P. Rule 6 (Time) and the related rules governing responsive
pleading and discovery response deadlines — in the same way the Office provides
electronic copies of portions of the Colorado Revised Statutes without charge.

I have been able to obtain the statutes I need directly from the Office's
published PDFs on leg.colorado.gov, including C.R.S. § 24-11-101, and I am
grateful that those are made available so straightforwardly. What I have not
been able to locate is a consolidated, current text of the Rules of Civil
Procedure from a free source: the Judicial Branch appears to publish individual
rule-change orders rather than a compiled current set, and the maintained text I
can find is on the LexisNexis-hosted Colorado Legal Resources site.

I am asking rather than assuming because I would prefer to work from a source
the State itself points to as authoritative, and because accuracy matters
particularly here — the material would be used to compute civil litigation
deadlines, where an incorrect rule text produces an incorrect date.

If the Office is not the right place for this request, I would be grateful for a
pointer to who is.

If there is a fee, a form to complete, or a preferred format, please let me know
and I will follow whatever process you use.

Thank you for your time.
