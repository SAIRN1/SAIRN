## 1. Relationship to Platform Terms

This SAIRNdental Service Agreement ("Agreement") supplements the SAIRN Platform Terms of Service, Data Processing Addendum, and Acceptable Use Policy (together, the "Platform Terms"), which apply in full to your use of SAIRNdental. This Agreement adds terms specific to SAIRNdental, SAIRN's dental practice management application. In the event of a direct conflict, this Agreement controls for SAIRNdental-specific matters.

## 2. Subscription and Licensed Practices

SAIRNdental is licensed on a monthly, auto-renewing subscription basis, per dental practice, with provider and staff seats as set out in your order form or account dashboard. A subscription covers one practice location unless upgraded to a multi-location or multi-seat plan.

## 3. AI-Assisted Features

SAIRNdental uses AI (Claude, via Anthropic) to assist with scheduling, documentation, and administrative review.

These features are decision-support tools. They do not diagnose, do not recommend treatment, and do not determine medical necessity. Every clinical judgment, treatment plan, and diagnosis remains the responsibility of your licensed providers.

Where SAIRNdental assists with procedure coding or coverage rules, its output is a reference against data you have entered and configured. It is not a certification that a claim is payable, correctly coded, or compliant. You remain responsible for the accuracy of every claim you submit.

## 4. No Clinical or Coding Advice from SAIRN; No Provider-Patient Relationship

SAIRN is a software provider. SAIRN is not a dental or medical practice, does not employ licensed clinicians in connection with this Platform, does not practice dentistry, and is not a certified professional coder or billing service.

Nothing generated by SAIRNdental — including AI output, coverage-rule results, or coding references — constitutes clinical advice, a diagnosis, a treatment recommendation, or a coding or billing determination from SAIRN. Use of the Platform creates no provider-patient relationship between SAIRN and any patient.

Your practice's licensed providers and qualified billing staff remain solely responsible for all clinical judgment, patient care, records accuracy, claim submission, and compliance with the professional-conduct, licensure, and payer rules applicable in your jurisdiction.

## 5. Patient Data and HIPAA

Patient data you enter into SAIRNdental — patient records, appointments, treatment and procedure records, charges, payments, accounts receivable, referrals, and clinical photographs — is Customer Data under the Data Processing Addendum, and constitutes protected health information ("PHI") under HIPAA.

You are the covered entity. SAIRN, in storing and processing PHI on your behalf, is a business associate.

**A separate Business Associate Agreement must be executed between you and SAIRN before any PHI is entered into SAIRNdental.** This Agreement does not create, replace, or satisfy that requirement. Where this Agreement and the executed BAA differ on the handling of PHI, the BAA controls.

You remain responsible for determining what patient information is appropriate to store in a third-party system, for your own HIPAA compliance obligations including the Security Rule and any required risk analysis, and for patient notice and authorization where your jurisdiction requires it.

## 6. Provider Credentials and Scheduling

SAIRNdental tracks provider credentials, licence and certification expiry, operatory assignment, and provider hours. These are recordkeeping and scheduling aids computed from data you enter.

SAIRNdental does not verify a credential against any licensing board, primary source, or registry, and an expiry warning is not a compliance certification. Credential verification, payer enrolment, and scope-of-practice compliance remain your responsibility.

## 7. Service Availability

SAIRN uses commercially reasonable efforts to keep SAIRNdental available. Standard pricing tiers do not include a guaranteed uptime SLA. Any SLA commitment must be set out in a separate written addendum.

## 8. Payment Terms

Subscription fees are billed monthly via credit card auto-billing. You may cancel at any time; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, with 30 days' notice requested where practical, per the Platform Terms.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

SAIRN's total liability arising out of or related to this Agreement is limited to the fees you paid SAIRN for SAIRNdental in the three (3) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim, consistent with the Platform Terms.

## 10. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Ohio, consistent with the Platform Terms.
