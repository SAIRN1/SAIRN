## 1. Purpose

This Revision No. 1 ("Revision") revises the SAIRN Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP"), which supplements the SAIRN Platform Terms of Service ("Terms"). Capitalized terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Terms.

The AUP's health-data obligations currently name SAIRNcare and SAIRNvet only. SAIRNdental processes dental patient records, and SAIRNsenior processes home-care client health records. Both require the same obligations. This Revision adds them and makes no other change.

This Revision is issued under Section 8 of the AUP (Changes to This Policy).

## 2. Revision to the Plain-Language Summary

The fifth bullet of the AUP's Plain-Language Summary is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> Specific rules apply to alcohol/retail sales features (SAIRNgrounds) and health-related data (SAIRNcare, SAIRNvet, SAIRNdental, SAIRNsenior) — see below.

## 3. Revision to Section 5 (Healthcare and Animal-Health Data)

Section 5 of the AUP, including its heading, is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> **5. Healthcare and Animal-Health Data (SAIRNcare, SAIRNvet, SAIRNdental, SAIRNsenior)**
>
> If you use SAIRNcare, SAIRNvet, SAIRNdental, or SAIRNsenior, additional obligations apply.
>
> - You must not enter protected health information (PHI) into SAIRNcare, SAIRNdental, or SAIRNsenior, or an equivalent covered workflow into SAIRNvet, before executing a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with SAIRN as described in the DPA;
> - You remain solely responsible for clinical judgment and care decisions; AI-generated diagnostic suggestions, drug-dosage calculations, treatment recommendations, or care-plan output require review and sign-off by a licensed veterinary, dental, medical, or nursing professional, as applicable to your practice, before acting on them;
> - You must not use the Platform to bypass controlled-substance recordkeeping, witness, or reporting requirements applicable in your jurisdiction;
> - You must not use the Platform to record, alter, or certify a visit, service, or encounter that did not occur, or to misstate its time, duration, or attendance. This applies in particular to electronic visit verification records in SAIRNsenior and to appointment and treatment records in SAIRNdental.

## 4. No Other Changes

Except as expressly revised above, the AUP remains in full force and effect and is unchanged. This Revision forms part of the AUP and supplements the Terms as the AUP does. In the event of a conflict between this Revision and the AUP, this Revision controls as to the sections it revises.

For the avoidance of doubt, this Revision does not constitute, create, or substitute for a Business Associate Agreement.

## 5. Reporting Abuse

To report suspected abuse or a security concern related to the Platform, contact SAIRN at michael@sairn.com.
