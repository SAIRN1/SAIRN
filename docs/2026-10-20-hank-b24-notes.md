## ITEM 3 — THE DUPLICATE OSHA CASE NUMBER. **REPRODUCED, FIXED, ARMED.**

### WHERE IT IS ISSUED

`sairnbiz.html` `sbNextCaseNo(list, year)` — `max(case_no for that year) + 1`
over `ld('sb_incidents', [])`, which is **one browser's localStorage**.
`saveIncident()` calls it and stores the result on the row.

> **CONCEPT, stated before the fix.** An OSHA Form 300 case number must be
> unique within its establishment-year **wherever it was issued**. The number
> that is already printed is **not silently rewritten** — the collision is
> **detected and shown in the column that is wrong**, because a duplicate
> Column A is a records problem a human resolves.

### THE REPRODUCTION, AND IT RAN RED BEFORE THE FIX

`api/b24-osha-caseno-collision.test.js` lifts the **real** functions out of
`sairnbiz.html` by brace matching and drives them in a `vm`:

* **A1** two devices with their own empty store **both issue case number 1** for
  2026 — neither is wrong on its own evidence.
* **A2** `sb_incidents` is in `SB_SYNCED` and `sbServerWinsMerge()` matches rows
  **by `id`** (`'IN' + Date.now().toString(36)`, per device). After the merge
  **both rows are present and both print as `2026-1`.** The merge had no reason
  to notice: its identity is `id`, and the two ids are genuinely different
  records.
* **A3** …and the YTD count is **correct at 2**. That is why nothing caught it:
  **the 300A totals are right while Column A is wrong**, and an inspector reads
  both.
* **B1 FAILED BEFORE THE FIX** — a second, narrower hole on **one** device. The
  year test was `String(r.year)!==String(year)`, so a row whose `year` field was
  absent was **invisible to the max scan** and the next save re-issued a number
  that row already held.

### THE FIX, THREE PARTS

1. `sbNextCaseNo` falls back to **the year of the row's `date`** when `year` is
   absent — not `r.year||…`, because a stored year of `0` is not a year and the
   `||` spelling would silently prefer the date in a case nobody would test.
2. **`sbCaseNoCollisions(list)`** — the `<year>-<case_no>` values held by more
   than one row, **one per duplicated number** rather than one per colliding
   pair; a row with **no** case number is not reported, because that is a
   different defect and *"duplicate --"* sends a reader looking for a number
   neither row has.
3. `rIncidents()` marks the offending **Column A cell** with
   *"⚠ DUPLICATE case number — another entry in 2026 also holds 1…"*. In Column
   A rather than a banner elsewhere: the column that is wrong is where a reader
   transcribing onto Form 300 is already looking. **Nothing renumbers.**

**17 arms → 25 with the ablations. 0 failed.**

### THE RESIDUAL, NAMED

Two devices **both offline at the moment of issue** can still mint the same
number, and nothing client-side can prevent it. The complete fix is a
**server-side per-year sequence** in `api/sd-data.js` — **cody's file, routed.**
Arm **E1** asserts the source says so.

### AND IT BROKE A GREEN SUITE, WHICH IS REPORTED RATHER THAN QUIETLY PATCHED

`tests/sairnbiz_incidents_kpi.js` lifts `rIncidents` into a `vm` context and now
lacked its new dependency: **nine arms died on `sbCaseNoCollisions is not
defined`** (25/25 → 16/25). Fixed by lifting the dependency too.
**A `typeof === 'function'` guard in the app was rejected**: in the browser both
functions are in the same file, so such a guard could only ever fire in a
harness that lifted one and not the other — and it would make that harness
report the render as fine while the warning never appeared. A missing dependency
in a lift is the harness's defect, not the app's to paper over. **25/25
restored.**
