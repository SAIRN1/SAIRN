**WHY NOTHING IS SEEDED.**
- **Every deadline row runs through Rule 6, and Rule 6 is not unamended since 2018.**
  - Its computation text is unchanged, but its legal-holiday list, which decides when a deadline rolls, changed in 2022 and again in 2024.
  - The 2022 order is missing from the official list, and I could not find it.
  - So the instruction ("seed only what's provably unamended") excludes the computation standard itself. Rows for 33/34/36/59 cannot be computed without it.
- **Each of the two official records is shown to have gaps:**
  - the order list omits 22-06 and the 2022 Rule 6 order;
  - the posted rules lag the orders: Rule 12 has no trace of 25-01.
- So "no order found" for 33/34/36/59 is strong evidence — the two records would have to miss the same amendment — but it is not proof.

**WHAT WOULD UNBLOCK IT:**
1. **A decision (Michael):** may the computation standard come from the CURRENT Rule 6? Its current text is fully known from the 2025 compile, the posted rule and order 24-02, and the only post-2018 changes are to the holiday list. If yes, then:
   - rows for 33, 34, 36 and 59 can be seeded on the current Rule 6 and the current (a)(6) holiday list;
   - 12(a) can be seeded from the 25-01 text, whose periods are unchanged.
2. **Or the 2022 Rule 6 promulgation order itself.** The Superior Court Rules Committee or the Clerk holds it. With it, every Rule 6 amendment since 2018 is in hand as a primary order.
3. **Rule 59 is not seeded under either route:** the DC appeal-time interplay was not read this batch.
