## 4 (stated first). The concept that decided item 1
> **A sync is real only when something on the other end reads what was written. With no reader, a
> write is not a sync, and an endpoint that answers 200 to it reports a success that did not happen.**

## 1. A full-sync endpoint — NOT BUILT, and why, from the repo rather than from preference
* **Nothing reads the bridge.** `api/bridge.js` header: *"So bridge_data is, today, written and never
  read."* Its read action (`pull`) was **removed on 2026-09-17** because it served one tenant's jobs,
  invoices and employees to an unauthenticated GET keyed on a raw licence key; the note says the read
  side, when built, "should key on license_hash and require a session". No app file references the
  bridge for reading (`grep bridge_data` over every `*.html`: comments only).
* **This exact feature was deleted once already for this reason.** `stonedesk.html` ~:30100:
  `syncToSAIRNdesign()` "pushed a job snapshot to ?action=push … Nothing reads bridge_data … and its
  success path even toasted 'Synced to SAIRNdesign'." Removed 2026-08-24.
* **The page's own copy was corrected for the same reason** (~:19736): it used to promise "One click
  syncs payroll, GL entries, job orders, and project data across your entire suite" and was cut
  because "that described a capability that does not exist".
* **A write would also destroy data.** `push` is latest-wins on one row per licence
  (~:20417: "each send REPLACES the previous one in bridge_data"), and the one live caller,
  `crSendToBridge`, queues expense entries in that row. A sync that pushed a snapshot would overwrite it.

So a `full-sync` action that returned 200 would make the button "succeed" at sending data to nothing,
and would bring back the false-success b29 removed, one layer down. **What a real sync needs**, and why
it is more than a batch: (a) a read side keyed on `license_hash` behind a session, (b) per-kind rows
instead of one latest-wins row, (c) at least one app that actually reads it, (d) a decision about which
data may cross between apps for one tenant. The button meanwhile tells the truth ("Sync failed (HTTP
400) -- nothing was synced", b29).

**Recommendation:** remove the "Sync All Apps" button (CSV export is the route that works), or
commission (a)–(d) as a scoped design. Either is Michael's call; nothing was changed.
