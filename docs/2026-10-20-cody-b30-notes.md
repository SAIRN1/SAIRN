## 1. THE CONCEPTS, STATED BEFORE ANY CODE (item 5)

| item | the concept |
|---|---|
| **1. the cap gate** | A payload cap belongs to the **(resource, action) pair** that writes, because *which verb writes* is a fact about the handler and *how big a row may be* is a fact about the table — and the gate asked **neither**. |
| **2. the dangling citation** | A citation is a claim about a commit that **other clones can resolve**; one kept alive by a local reflog names nothing anywhere else, so it is re-seated by matching the record's **own measurements** against a surviving commit, never by picking the nearest subject. |
| **3. the split** | An append-only log is split on an **entry boundary**, and the cut is only safe if the entry **set** is provably unchanged — so it is verified by hashing the entry multiset before and after, never by comparing byte counts. |

## 2. ITEM 1 — THE GATE COVERED ONE VERB CLASS OF THIRTY-EIGHT

cody's own b29 finding, closed here. `api/sd-data.js` read
`if (action === 'write')`, so **every one of the 38 declared verbs across 77
resources bypassed the payload cap entirely.** `rf_invoices` carries
`rfinv_data_size <= 262144` and writes through `issue`, `add_payment`,
`reconcile_claim` and `gl_export`: not under-capped, **uncapped**.

**THE GATE IS NOW FAIL-CLOSED ON THE VERB, and that ordering is the whole
safety argument.** It caps **every action except `read`**, rather than every
action on a declared write-class list — so a verb misclassified in
`ACTION_CLASS` is **still capped**, and a wrong entry there costs a comment
rather than a hole. `read` is excluded because a read payload is a filter: it
writes no row, and capping it would be a new restriction on something that was
never the defect. Arm **G4** is that boundary.

**Three-step resolution, one function (`payloadCapFor`):** the
`(resource, action)` entry, then b28/b29's per-resource entry, then 64KB. Step 2
is why the six resources b28 and b29 capped now keep those caps on **every**
verb instead of only on `write` — arm **G5**.

**THE ONE PLACE THE "THIS BREAKS NOTHING" ARGUMENT FAILS IS `rt_routes`, AND IT
IS WHY STEP 1 EXISTS.** `upsert` and `optimize` carry the whole stop **list**,
not one row: `api/_lib/routes.js` permits 200 stops (`:464`) with 500-character
addresses (`:470`), so a full route is **~109KB measured** of legitimate payload
and the 64KB default would have refused it. **That is the regression b29
declined to ship blind**, and arm **R1** is the real 200-stop payload. The
ceiling is derived — `RT_STOPS_MAX * RT_STOP_WORST_BYTES + 32768` — not typed,
and **R2** proves it is a cap and not an exemption. `reorder` carries a `seq`
array of ids rather than stops and is deliberately absent; **R3** asserts that,
because *"we forgot reorder"* and *"reorder does not need it"* look identical in
a table.

### 2.1 The class map is a DECLARATION, and arm M1 is why that is honest

The brief asked for the split into write-class and compute-only. **The honest
first attempt was to DERIVE it** — scan `api/sd-data.js` for each verb's
`action === '<verb>'` branch and test the span for a write method. **That scan
is not trustworthy and the measurement said so:** it reported `consolidate` as
having **no branch at all**, and `reserve` and `release_retainage` as
compute-only, which is wrong for a verb whose whole job is to reserve a slab.
The span ends at the next `if (resource ===` boundary and several branches put
the write after a nested resource test.

So `ACTION_CLASS` in `api/_resources/index.js` is declared by hand, with a
**load-time completeness assertion that throws**, naming any unclassified verb
— because the map's silence would otherwise read as *"it computes"*. And arm
**M1 runs the rejected derivation anyway** and asserts the disagreements are
**exactly the three pinned verbs**: a new disagreement means the scan has a new
blind spot or a class changed, and a disagreement that **disappears** means the
pin is stale. Neither can happen quietly.

**18 arms, 7 red under ablation.**

### 3.2 → fourth: the pasteable change

Two fields in the record whose `commit` is `ce15ddaa6a3c`:

```json

"subject": "b27(cody): rt_routes made reachable, three routed role changes, and three routings that were incomplete"
```

**The `subject` must move with the `commit`** — the register's own check matches
on subject as a fallback, so leaving the old wording would make the record

## 4. ITEM 3 — TWO REFUSALS FOUND THE SHAPE, AND THE SECOND IS THE FINDING

`SAIRN-ACTIVE-WORK-cody.md` was **795,238 bytes** against a 400,000 ceiling.

| attempt | result |
|---|---|
| 1 — one archive part | **REFUSED**: no single cut leaves both files under the ceiling. fourth hit the identical wall on 2026-10-12 and also went to two parts. |
| 2 — N parts, everything before the first `## ` treated as a header | **REFUSED** on *"not even one section fits the live file"* — because that "header" is **425,249 bytes**. |
| 3 — three regions | **WRITTEN** |

**THE SECOND REFUSAL IS THE FINDING: this log has TWO FORMATS IN ONE FILE.**
The oldest **88 entries are bullets** in the original format (423,683 bytes);
the newer **127 are `## <date> (Cody) -- <subject>` sections** (369,891 bytes).
A script that had guessed instead of refusing would have written a live file
still over the ceiling and reported success.

**So the bullets archive and the sections stay live** — which is both the
correct size split and the correct editorial one, because the recent work is
what a fresh session needs.

    before   795,238 bytes, 215 entries, multiset 23779075228a6557
    after    SAIRN-ACTIVE-WORK-cody-closed-1.md   395,093   79 entries
             SAIRN-ACTIVE-WORK-cody-closed-2.md    33,368    9 entries
             SAIRN-ACTIVE-WORK-cody.md           372,166  127 entries
             multiset 23779075228a6557, re-read FROM DISK over the union

**Byte counts would not have been enough** and that is written into each
archive header: two files that sum to the original size can still have lost one
entry and gained a copy of another. The write only happened because the
multiset matched, and the ceiling tool now reports **7** over-ceiling documents
where it reported 8.
