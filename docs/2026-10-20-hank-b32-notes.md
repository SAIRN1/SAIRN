## 1. The concept, before the code
> **A probe that authenticates as the wrong role proves nothing — before trusting any probe result,
> confirm it signs in as the actual role under test.**

Both live probes now call `whoami` after every login and compare the **employee id and role read from
the credential row** with the ones under test. A mismatch is **exit 2, COULD NOT RUN** — never a verdict.
The login reply is not trusted for this: it is what the endpoint chose to echo, `whoami` is what the lock
and the pay run actually read.
