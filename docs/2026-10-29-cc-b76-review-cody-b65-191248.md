licence fingerprint is case-sensitive, while `api/_lib/license.js:189` accepts a
case-corrected key as the same licence. Failure scenario: a legacy device whose

identity)**. Owner: **cody**. Suggested repair: fingerprint a normalised key on
both sides (`String(k).trim().toUpperCase()`), with an arm for case-only
difference → no ask.
