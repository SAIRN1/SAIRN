THE MANIFEST STATES ITS OWN LIMIT IN ITS OWN TEXT: a size match is weaker than
a hash match, a same-length corruption passes it, that is a third state and
not a pass. It also records the lag finding so the next reader does not
re-diagnose it.

briefly returned two on one branch and three on another, which is the next

took. The ablation stub returns the same shape and hands back what it MEANT to
write -- which is what makes it an ablation of the read-back rather than of
the write.

The manifest is itself written through write_verified: a manifest nobody
checked is a second unverified claim about the first. A board that publishes
while its manifest does not is reported as its own state -- files verified,
