================================================================================

MICHAEL'S DECISION: "Paralegal: goes back on the IOLTA reconcile list as
PREPARE-ONLY. The attorney or owner attests (same two-stage rule as trust
disbursement; a solo firm self-attests per the per-firm setting)."

producing a finished reconciliation that nothing records a lawyer as having

delegable under supervision. Two sentences, two sets.

So the grant went on a SECOND frozen null-prototype set,

Same shape as the trust disbursement two-stage, deliberately and not by analogy:
LAW_TRUST_PREPARE_ROLES is { paralegal: true } for the same reason, and
api/_lib/countersign.js's MODE_PREPARED_ONLY is the lock that holds it.

THE HALF THAT MAKES IT SAFE IS THE STAMP, NOT THE PERMISSION. Every reconcile
reply now carries:

    attested: false                      UNCONDITIONAL
    prepare_only: <true for a paralegal>
    prepared_by_role: <the session's role>
    attestation_required_from: ['attorney','owner']   derived from
                                                      LAW_BAR_ADMITTED_ROLES
    attestation_recorded_by: 'POST /api/law-attest'

`attested: false` is unconditional INCLUDING AN OWNER'S OWN RUN, because running
the comparison and attesting to it are two acts even when one person does both.
`attested: recMayRun` is the natural-looking one-liner and is the lie the stamp
exists to prevent -- an owner's run is a comparison by somebody who is ALLOWED
to attest and has not yet. Arm P4 is that case.

`prepared_by_role` AND NOT `prepared_by`: the employee id is already the
caller's own, and adding it would put a named person into a payload the client
renders beside a trust balance. The ROLE is what decides whether an attestation
is still owed.

THE SOLO-FIRM CLAUSE IS ALREADY BUILT and is referenced, not rebuilt:
api/_lib/law-attest.js's named single-signer mode for a firm with exactly one
active owner/attorney, stored as signer_mode on the row so the record never
claims two people looked when one did (hank, b23).

LAW_RECONCILE_ATTESTATION gained a frozen prepare_only_contract carrying the
five conditions the grant rests on -- who may run, who may attest, the solo-firm
mode, the unconditional stamp, and which arms hold it -- because prose in a
handoff would have been the fourth place those conditions were written and the
first to go stale. Its `gap` was RESTATED: two of its three clauses stopped
being true, and the one that matters has not -- the attestation table is still
unprovisioned, so no attestation can be RECORDED yet. The grant was made anyway
because the reply is stamped regardless, so an unprovisioned table cannot be
