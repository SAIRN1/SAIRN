ITEM 3 -- THE sen_visits clock_flags MERGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
api/sd-data.js assigned `visitData.clock_flags = clockCheck.flags`, and a
visit's two halves arrive as TWO SEPARATE WRITES. checkClockEvent only reports
on the clock fields present in THIS payload, so the clock-out's write REPLACED
the clock-in's flags: a LATE_REPLAY on the way in vanished with nothing saying
it had existed. Found in cody's b8 Tier A review, not by any checker.

It only showed when BOTH halves were flagged -- the audit case. With a clean
clock-out the array is empty, the old guard never fired, and the first flag
survived by luck.

THE SUPERSEDED SET IS TAKEN FROM THE PAYLOAD, NOT FROM THE NEW FLAGS, and that
is the whole correctness of the fix. A field this payload re-reported has been
re-checked, so its stale flag must go EVEN IF the re-check produced none --
otherwise a corrected clock-in keeps a LATE_REPLAY forever. A field the payload
did not mention was not checked, so its flag is kept untouched.

The key stays ABSENT when there is nothing to say: an empty array would be a
visible change to every clean EVV write, and api/sd-data-sen-evv-clock.test.js
:200 asserts the key is not present on a clean one.

CLOCK_FIELDS hoisted out of checkClockEvent in api/_lib/sen-evv-clock.js and
EXPORTED rather than re-listed in sd-data.js. Two copies would drift the day a
third clock field is added, and the merge would then keep a stale flag on it
forever -- cross-domain convention 7.
