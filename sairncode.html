Privacy & Data Handling

Where your data goes

Entries you create in SAIRNcode (claims, denials, authorizations, eligibility history and the rest) are stored two places: in this browser, and — once you are signed in — on SAIRN's server, scoped to your practice's license so other practices cannot read them.

AI features and clinical notes

When you use an AI feature — the assistant, Suggest Codes From a Note, the rule-based scrub, or the 2026 code lookup — the text you submit is sent to Anthropic, which operates the Claude model, and is processed on their systems to produce the response. If you paste a clinical note, that note's contents go with it.

Only send what the task needs. A note stripped of the patient's name and identifiers usually produces the same coding answer as one that includes them.

The rule-based scrub and the 2026 code lookup additionally perform a live web search to ground their answers in current CMS/AMA guidance. The codes and terms you enter form the search query.

What is recorded about AI use

Every AI request is recorded in an audit log: who made it, which feature, when, how much text was submitted, and whether it succeeded. The note text, the prompt, and the AI's response are deliberately not stored in that log — recording them would create a larger, permanent copy of the very data the log exists to make accountable.

Third parties involved
  • Anthropic — processes AI requests, including any note text you submit.
  • Vercel — hosts this application and its API.
  • Supabase — stores your practice's saved records.
  • Stedi — only if your practice configures eligibility checks. Those requests go through your own Stedi account, not a SAIRN-owned one, so that relationship stays between your practice and Stedi.
Your control over this data

Every record has a delete control, and deletion is enforced server-side (a Compliance Admin session is required). Signing out clears your session from this device.

Stated honestly: there is no one-click "delete everything for this practice" function yet. Records persist until someone deletes them — see the retention setting below for what that means.

Data retention policy
Nothing is automatically deleted today. This setting records your practice's stated retention policy so it is written down and consistent. It does not purge, expire, or delete any record, and no scheduled deletion exists anywhere in SAIRNcode. Records persist until a person deletes them.

Medical billing records are generally subject to long retention obligations that vary by state, by payer contract, and by record type. SAIRNcode does not attempt to determine yours — set this to match the policy your practice has actually adopted.

The minimum is 10 years and cannot be set lower — enforced on the server, not just here. That floor exists precisely because no purge is built yet: whatever deletion mechanism is eventually built will inherit this value, long after anyone remembers choosing it.
