📖 LCD / NCD Coverage Lookup — Medical Necessity Reference
LCD vs NCD: National Coverage Determinations (NCDs) apply across all Medicare Administrative Contractors (MACs). Local Coverage Determinations (LCDs) are contractor-specific. When both exist, the more restrictive applies. Coverage affects: whether Medicare pays, required diagnosis codes, documentation requirements, and medical necessity criteria.
